About

Made by a dance creator, for dance creators

Video Clipper started as a way to stop spending whole evenings editing and posting the videos from each dance night.

Why we built it

Social dance nights produce hours of footage. Sharing it means cutting dozens of clips, making a cover for each, rewriting the same caption with a new part number and posting to four apps, one by one.

Video Clipper automates the repetitive parts so creators can focus on dancing and on the community, while keeping full control over what gets published and when.

Our principles

  • You decide. Nothing is published without an explicit action from you.
  • Official APIs only. We integrate with each network through its official, approved APIs.
  • Respect for dancers. Covers never hide faces and dancers are credited in every caption.
  • Privacy first. No ads, no data selling, encrypted tokens, easy deletion.

Who runs Video Clipper

Video Clipper is developed and operated by Hugo Fulgor, an independent developer based in Mexico.

Status

Private beta, used by invited creators. New features are added based on their feedback.
