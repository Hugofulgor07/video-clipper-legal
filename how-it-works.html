How it works

From recording to published clip

This is the full workflow inside Video Clipper. Every publication starts with an explicit action from you.

Sign in

Sign in with your Google account. Video Clipper only receives your name, email address and profile photo.

Connect your social accounts

On the Accounts screen, connect each network with its official login page (TikTok Login Kit, Facebook Login, Instagram Login and Google for YouTube). You review and approve the permissions on the network’s own screen. You can disconnect any account at any time.

Upload a video

Upload a long recording from your computer or iPhone. Video Clipper analyses the music and proposes short vertical clips.

Review clips and covers

Each clip comes with a cover built from your brand kit. Change the cover frame, the “Part N” label, its position or rotation, or remove clips you don’t want.

Write the caption

Enter the dancer’s name and profiles, the venue, the event date and your hashtags, or paste the caption you always use. Each clip receives its own part number.

Choose networks and times

Select the networks for each clip and publish now or schedule. Suggested times spread your clips evenly through the day.

Follow the results

The calendar and the Publications screen show the status on every network and a link to each live post.

Publishing to TikTok

Video Clipper uses the TikTok Content Posting API. There are two ways a clip reaches TikTok:

Upload to your inbox (draft)

With video.upload, the clip is sent to your TikTok inbox. TikTok notifies you and you finish the post in the TikTok app: edit, add sounds, write the caption and choose who can see it.

Direct Post

With video.publish, before posting you see the connected account’s nickname and choose the privacy level (there is no preselected value), whether to allow comments, Duet and Stitch, and whether the post promotes your own or a third party’s brand. You confirm TikTok’s Music Usage Confirmation and nothing is posted until you press Publish. Afterwards TikTok may take a few minutes to process the video.

Disconnecting

Press “Disconnect” next to any network on the Accounts screen and its access tokens are deleted from Video Clipper immediately. You can also remove access from the network: in TikTok, go to Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Deleting your data

Delete individual videos and clips inside the app, or ask us to delete your whole account. See Data deletion for details.
