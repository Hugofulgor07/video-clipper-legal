TikTok integration

Built on TikTok’s official APIs

You connect your TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit. Video Clipper never asks for your password and only acts when you press Publish or Schedule.

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic) to show which account is connected.
  • Upload to inbox (video.upload): the clip arrives in your TikTok inbox as a draft so you finish the post in the TikTok app.
  • Direct Post (video.publish): you choose the privacy level, comments, Duet and Stitch settings and any commercial content disclosure before posting.
  • Disconnect anytime: your TikTok tokens are deleted immediately.

Your content stays yours

  • We only publish the clips you select, to the accounts you connected.
  • Access tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM).
  • No ads, no data selling, no use of your videos to train AI models.
  • Delete videos, disconnect networks or close your account whenever you want.

How it works

From a two-hour recording to scheduled posts in four steps.

Upload your video

Drop a long video from your computer or phone. HDR and iPhone footage are supported.

Review the clips

Video Clipper proposes the clips and their covers. Change the cover frame, the text or the order if you want.

Write the caption once

Dancer, venue, date and hashtags. Each clip gets “Part 1”, “Part 2”… automatically.

Publish or schedule

Pick the networks and times. You can follow every post from the calendar.
