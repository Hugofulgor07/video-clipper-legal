Video Clipper

Cut dance videos. Publish short clips.

A web app for creators who film social dance nights and want ready-to-post clips for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and Instagram — with captions and scheduled publishing.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What it does

  • Upload a long dance video from your computer.
  • Automatically generate short clips based on music energy.
  • Create vertical covers suitable for TikTok / Reels / Shorts.
  • Write one caption template (dancer, venue, date, hashtags).
  • Connect social accounts and publish or schedule clips.

TikTok integration

  • Login / connect: users authorize Video Clipper with TikTok Login Kit (user.info.basic).
  • Upload draft: Content Posting API video.upload sends a draft to the creator inbox when needed.
  • Direct Post: Content Posting API video.publish posts on the user’s behalf after they choose to publish in the app.
  • Users can disconnect TikTok anytime; we delete stored tokens.
  • We only act when the user explicitly clicks Publish or Schedule.

Who it’s for

  • Dance creators and social event organizers (personal/production tool).
  • Not a consumer social network and not for scraping or spam.
