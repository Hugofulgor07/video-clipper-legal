Data deletion

You can delete your content, disconnect platforms or delete your whole account at any time.

Delete videos and clips

Open Videos in the app, choose a video and delete it. Its clips and covers are deleted with it. If some of its clips are still scheduled, cancel those posts first.

Disconnect a platform

Open Accounts and press Disconnect next to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube. The access tokens and account details for that platform are deleted from Video Clipper immediately.

You can also revoke access from the platform itself:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → Video Clipper → Remove access.
  • Facebook / Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites (Facebook) or Website permissions → Apps and websites (Instagram) → remove Video Clipper.
  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Video Clipper → Remove access.

Delete your account and all data

  1. Send an email to hugofulgor@gmail.com from the address you use to sign in, with the subject “Delete my Video Clipper account”.
  2. We confirm the request by replying to that address.
  3. Within 30 days we delete your account, videos, clips, covers, captions, brand kit, platform tokens and publication records, and email you when it is done.

Technical logs that may contain your IP address are deleted automatically within 90 days. Posts that are already published on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube are not deleted by Video Clipper; remove them directly on each platform.
