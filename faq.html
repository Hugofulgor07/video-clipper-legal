Frequently asked questions

General

What is Video Clipper?

A web app that turns long dance videos into short vertical clips with covers and captions, and publishes or schedules them to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

Who is it for?

Dance creators and social dance organisers who film events and post the best moments as a series of short clips. It is a creator tool, not a social network.

How do I get access?

Video Clipper is in private beta. Send a request from the Contact page and we will reply by email.

Which devices are supported?

Any modern browser on desktop, plus iPhone and Android. On iPhone you can add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

Publishing

Will Video Clipper post anything without my permission?

No. Clips are only published when you press Publish or Schedule for that clip and network. You can cancel any scheduled post before it goes out.

How does publishing to TikTok work?

You connect TikTok with TikTok Login Kit. Clips are sent with the TikTok Content Posting API, either to your TikTok inbox as a draft that you finish in the TikTok app, or by Direct Post after you choose the privacy level and interaction settings. See How it works.

Does Video Clipper see my TikTok password?

No. You sign in on TikTok’s own page and TikTok gives Video Clipper an access token limited to the permissions you approve.

Can I edit the caption for each network?

Yes. You write one caption template and Video Clipper adapts mentions for each network. You can edit the text before scheduling.

What happens if a post fails?

The post is marked as failed with the reason given by the network, and you can retry it or publish it manually.

Content

What kind of videos can I upload?

Common formats such as MP4 and MOV, including HDR and Dolby Vision video recorded on iPhone. You must own the video or have permission to publish it.

Who is responsible for the content I publish?

You are. Make sure you have the rights to the video and music and that the people in it agree to be published, and follow each network’s community guidelines.

Privacy and data

What data does Video Clipper store?

Your Google sign-in profile, the videos and clips you upload, your caption settings and the access tokens of the networks you connect, encrypted. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Do you sell my data or use it for ads?

No. Your data is only used to run the service. We don’t show ads and don’t use your videos to train AI models.

How do I disconnect TikTok or another network?

Press “Disconnect” next to the network on the Accounts screen; its tokens are deleted immediately. You can also revoke access from TikTok in Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

How do I delete my account and data?

Follow the steps on the Data deletion page. Requests are completed within 30 days.
