Features

Everything you need to post a dance night

Video Clipper covers the whole workflow: cutting, designing covers, writing captions, scheduling and publishing.

Editing

Turn long recordings into clips that look good on every phone.

✂️

Automatic clip detection

The audio is analysed to find natural cut points in the music, so each clip starts and ends at a sensible moment.

📐

Vertical 9:16 output

Clips are exported in the vertical format used by TikTok, Reels and Shorts, keeping the dancers in frame.

🌈

HDR and iPhone footage

Videos recorded in HDR or Dolby Vision on iPhone keep their color, so clips look the same as the original.

⏱️

Speed and length controls

Adjust where a clip starts and ends, change its speed slightly or reorder the clips before publishing.

🔢

Series numbering

Clips from the same night are numbered “Part 0/1, 2, 3…” so viewers can find the rest on your profile.

🎬

End card

Optionally add a short “Part N+1 on my profile” invitation in the last seconds of each clip.

Covers and branding

A recognisable look in the feed, without manual design work.

🖼️

Brand kit

Upload your logo once and pick fonts, colors and the position of your @handle. Every cover follows the same template.

🙂

Face-aware layout

Text and logos are placed automatically so they never cover a dancer’s face.

🔲

Every format

Covers are generated in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 so they fit profile grids and feeds on each network.

Captions

Write the details once and let each clip get its own caption.

✍️

Caption template

Dancer, venue, map link, event date and hashtags. Paste your usual caption and only the part number changes.

🏷️

Per-network details

Mentions are written as @handles on their own network and as profile links on the others.

🔗

“Follow me on” block

Optionally add links to your other social profiles at the end of every caption.

Scheduling and publishing

Post on time, on every network, from one screen.

🕒

Suggested times

Choose how many clips you want per day and Video Clipper spreads them evenly through your posting hours.

📅

Calendar

See today, tomorrow and the next weeks: time, title, part number and the status on each network.

🚀

Official integrations

TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API), Instagram and Facebook (Meta Graph API) and YouTube (YouTube Data API).

✅

Status tracking

Each post shows whether it is scheduled, uploading, published or needs attention, with a link to the live post.

🔔

Notifications

Optional messages to your own Telegram chat when a clip is published or needs you to finish it in an app.

✋

You stay in control

Nothing is published until you press Publish or Schedule. Cancel or reschedule any post before it goes out.
