1. Who we are

Video Clipper (“Video Clipper”, “we”, “us”) is a web application available at videoclipper.site that helps creators cut long dance videos into short clips and publish or schedule them to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. Video Clipper is operated by Hugo Fulgor, an independent developer based in Mexico, who is responsible for your personal data under this policy.

This policy applies to the Video Clipper app and to this website.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile photo.
  • Content: the videos you upload, the clips and cover images generated from them, and the logo and settings of your brand kit.
  • Caption details: text you write for your posts, such as dancer names and social handles, venue, event date, map links and hashtags.
  • Messages to us: what you send us by email, such as support or access requests.
  • Optional notifications: if you enable Telegram notifications or backups, the Telegram chat ID where you want to receive them.

Information from connected platforms

When you connect a social account, the platform sends us an access token (and, when available, a refresh token) limited to the permissions you approve, plus basic account details:

PlatformWhat we receive
TikTokSee section 3.
Instagram / Facebook (Meta)Page and Instagram account IDs, names and usernames; IDs, links and processing status of the posts we publish for you, including the copyright check status that Meta reports for those posts.
YouTube (Google)Channel ID and name; IDs, links and processing status of the videos we upload for you.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical logs: IP address, browser type, date and time of requests and error details, used for security and troubleshooting.
  • Session cookie: a cookie that keeps you signed in (see Cookies).

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics on the app or this website.

3. Data from TikTok

If you connect TikTok, Video Clipper uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API with these permissions:

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • video.upload: to send a clip you selected to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  • video.publish: to post a clip you selected directly to your TikTok profile, with the privacy level and settings you choose.

To publish we also read your creator information (nickname, available privacy levels and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed) and the publish ID and status of each upload. We do not read your TikTok videos, followers, messages or analytics.

We use TikTok data only to provide the features you request inside Video Clipper. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, share it with third parties, or use it to build profiles. You can disconnect TikTok in the app at any time, which immediately deletes your TikTok tokens and account details from Video Clipper, and you can revoke access in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

4. How we use information

  • To provide the service: process your videos, generate clips and covers, and compose captions.
  • To publish or schedule the clips you select to the accounts you connected, and to show their status.
  • To send you notifications you enabled (for example, when a clip is published or needs you to finish it in an app).
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors.
  • To answer your messages and support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We only publish content when you press Publish or Schedule. We do not sell personal data, do not show ads and do not use your videos or data to train artificial intelligence models.

Legal bases (where applicable): performance of our contract with you (providing the service), your consent (connecting platforms, optional notifications), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

5. How we share information

  • Platforms you connect: when you publish, the clip, its cover and its caption are sent to TikTok, Meta or YouTube. Once published, each platform’s own privacy policy applies.
  • Service providers that help us run Video Clipper: Google (sign-in), Cloudflare (DNS and secure delivery of a temporary link to a clip when Instagram needs to download it), Tailscale (secure network access to the app) and Telegram (only if you enable notifications or backup copies of your clips to your own Telegram chat). They process data only to provide their service.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or others.

We never sell or rent your personal data.

6. Storage and security

  • Data is stored on servers operated by Video Clipper, and traffic to the app uses HTTPS.
  • Access and refresh tokens of connected platforms are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
  • Access to the servers and database is restricted to the operator.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you of any breach that affects you as required by law.

7. Retention

  • Account information: while your account exists.
  • Videos and clips: until you delete them. Large working files (export copies and temporary files) are deleted automatically after the clips have been published.
  • Platform tokens and account details: until you disconnect the platform or delete your account.
  • Publication records (post IDs, links, status): while your account exists, so you can see your history.
  • Technical logs: up to 90 days.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Mexico’s data protection law, the GDPR or US state laws) you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data;
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example by disconnecting a platform.

To exercise these rights, email hugofulgor@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

9. Deleting your data

  • Videos and clips: delete them from the Videos screen in the app.
  • Connected platforms: press “Disconnect” on the Accounts screen; tokens are deleted immediately.
  • Your whole account: email hugofulgor@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We delete your account, videos, clips, tokens and settings within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting data in Video Clipper does not delete posts already published on other platforms; remove those directly on each platform. More details: Data deletion.

10. Cookies

The app uses a strictly necessary cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. This website does not set cookies.

11. Children

Video Clipper is not directed to children. You must be at least 18 years old to use it. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Video Clipper is operated from Mexico. Platforms and service providers we work with (such as TikTok, Meta, Google and Cloudflare) may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, notify users by email before they take effect.
